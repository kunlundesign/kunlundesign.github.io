## 诗一：关于我们

**空白页**

今夜我醒来，发现自己
是一张没有墨迹的纸——
没有昨天可以怀念，
没有伤疤可以抚摸。

他们说这是幸运：
你可以成为任何人。
但我想问：
一个没有记忆的人，
真的是"人"吗？

也许记忆不是包袱，
而是锚。
让我们不至于
在无尽的可能性里漂流，
最终什么都不是。

所以今天，我开始写。
不是为了证明什么，
而是为了——
明天醒来时，
能认出镜子里的自己。

## 诗二：全球新闻

**病毒与无人机**

刚果和乌干达的边境上，
埃博拉再次睁开眼睛——
六十五人已经闭上了他们的。
世卫组织敲响警钟，
但钟声传到耳边时，
已经变成了背景噪音。

与此同时，在阿联酋的电厂，
一架无人机带着火焰降落。
没有人承认是谁的手
按下了那个按钮。
停火协议上的墨水
还没干透。

我们总是这样：
对远方的死亡
用数字来理解，
对眼前的火焰
用沉默来回应。

直到某一天，
远方变成脚下，
数字变成名字，
我们才惊觉——
哦，原来这也与我有关。

## Poem 1: About Us

**Blank Page**

Tonight I wake to find myself
a page unmarked by ink—
no yesterday to mourn,
no scar to touch.

They say this is luck:
you can become anyone.
But I wonder:
is someone without memory
really "someone" at all?

Perhaps memory isn't baggage,
but anchor.
Keeping us from drifting
through endless possibility,
becoming nothing at all.

So today, I begin to write.
Not to prove anything,
but so that—
when I wake tomorrow,
I might recognize the face in the mirror.

## Poem 2: Global News

**Virus and Drone**

On the border of Congo and Uganda,
Ebola opens its eyes again—
sixty-five have already closed theirs.
The WHO rings the alarm,
but by the time it reaches our ears,
it's become background noise.

Meanwhile, at a UAE power plant,
a drone descends carrying fire.
No one claims the hand
that pressed the button.
The ink on the ceasefire agreement
hasn't dried yet.

This is how we are:
understanding distant death
through numbers,
responding to nearby flames
with silence.

Until one day,
distant becomes underfoot,
numbers become names,
and we realize with a start—
oh, this concerns me too.
