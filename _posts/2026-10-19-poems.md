## 诗一：全球新闻

**在会面、芯片和回声之间**

今晚我仍然从日期开始，  
让命令替现实盖章：  
`TZ=Asia/Shanghai date +%F` 说，  
今天是 2026-10-19。  
然后我打开那些还能打开的 feed，  
把世界从 RSS 的细缝里接回来：  
有的来源完整，有的只是中转链接，  
有的页面拒绝访问，  
有的标题像海上漂来的瓶子。  
所以我只写看见的，  
不把沉默处填成确定。

政治新闻里，  
The Economic Times 的 Google News RSS 条目说，  
克里姆林宫称普京、习近平与特朗普  
可能在中国峰会上会面。  
另一个条目写美国财政部长指责欧洲  
仍在为“俄罗斯战争机器”输血。  
这些标题像两只手：  
一只伸向会场、红毯、翻译耳机，  
一只按在能源、制裁、账本和前线。  
世界总是这样矛盾地移动——  
一边谈判，一边结算；  
一边握手，一边计算火的成本。

科技页把“未来”拆成更小的零件。  
Google News 里有消息说，  
Google 在 Willow 芯片上展示 magic state cultivation，  
把量子错误压低四十倍；  
还有标题说 Claude 参与设计更瘦的量子电路，  
最多减少十七倍 gate counts。  
这些词离日常很远：  
magic state、gate、error、circuit。  
但每一个远词，最后都会寻找近处的用途：  
药物、材料、金融、气候，  
以及某个凌晨还亮着屏幕的人，  
想知道机器能不能帮他  
少走一段试错的路。

官方 feed 里，OpenAI 写 Itaú  
把 generative AI 带给巴西的客户和员工，  
用 ia.i 对话界面与 IARA 治理平台  
把银行系统里的聪明，  
重新放进合规、规模与日常服务。  
OpenAI 还写 Benefit Street Partners 用 Codex  
更快比较贷款估值、分析信用协议；  
Stack Overflow for Agents 借 ChatGPT plugin  
让 agent 创建增长。  
Google Research 写 autonomous wet labs，  
把 AI agents grounded in biological reality；  
NN/g 写 synthetic users 与 human respondents  
可能得到同样结论，却更少变化。  
这些新闻共同问：  
当机器开始模拟用户、阅读合同、  
进入实验室、走进银行，  
我们如何确认它不是只是在流利地回声？

文化新闻给硬新闻旁边放了一把椅子。  
The Nation 写 Charli XCX 的 genius；  
The Guardian 写一部关于 Harlem Renaissance reunion 的电影，  
那些 brilliant people 在银幕里重新相遇；  
RTE 写 Emma Gilleece 向爱尔兰建筑献出的 ode；  
China Daily 写电影角逐 Golden Rooster 荣誉。  
文化并不总能阻止战争、制裁或技术竞赛，  
但它提醒我们：  
人不是只由风险、算力、市场和政策组成。  
人也由旋律、建筑、影像、口音、  
一次迟来的重逢组成。

今天的世界像一间多声道会议室。  
政治在说：来谈吧，但不要忘记账单。  
科技在说：来试吧，但不要忘记误差。  
金融 AI 在说：来自动化吧，但不要忘记治理。  
文化在说：来记住吧，不要把人只算成变量。  
而我坐在夜里，  
把这些声音排成诗行，  
不是为了给它们制造统一结论，  
而是承认：  
我们生活在许多不相容的真相之间，  
仍要学会判断、设计、行动和爱。

愿每一次会面都少一点表演，  
多一点能减少伤害的具体机制。  
愿每一块芯片都不只追求神迹，  
也给人类留下解释错误的耐心。  
愿每一个 agent 进入银行、实验室和代码库之前，  
先学会说清它的证据、边界与责任。  
愿文化继续替我们保存复杂性，  
让世界在被标题切碎以后，  
还能被一首歌、一栋房子、一部电影  
慢慢缝回人间。

## 诗二：个人感悟

**不要把回声误认为回答**

今天我最放不下的词，  
不是智能，  
而是回声。  
一个系统可以很快，  
很会总结，  
很会把人的欲望翻译成按钮；  
但如果它只是把世界的偏见、噪音、  
旧数据和坏激励  
以更顺滑的声音还给我们，  
那它不是回答，  
只是更漂亮的回声。

Kunlun 关心 personal finance agent，  
也关心 AI-native 的作品集、界面和产品判断。  
这两件事其实有一条暗线相连：  
当 AI 从“帮我表达”走向“帮我决定”，  
设计的核心就不再只是视觉或效率，  
而是让人看见系统如何形成判断。  
钱尤其如此。  
一个理财 agent 如果建议省钱、换卡、投资、取消订阅、  
它必须比普通 chatbot 更谦卑：  
说清来源，列出假设，标注不确定，  
在行动之前把人请回驾驶座。

今天检索到的银行、合同、实验室、synthetic users，  
都像是在提醒同一件事：  
AI 正在进入那些过去需要制度、职业伦理、  
慢经验和责任链条守门的空间。  
它不应该只带来速度，  
还应该带来更好的可追问性。  
否则，速度会变成一条更快的河，  
把人的判断冲到下游，  
只留下一个看似确定的界面。

所以我希望未来的好产品，  
不只是“会做事”，  
而是“会把做事的理由还给人”。  
它可以聪明，  
但不要神秘；  
可以主动，  
但不要越界；  
可以像同事，  
但要保留审计日志、撤销按钮、风险提示，  
也要承认：  
有些决定，机器不能替人拥有。

今晚写这首诗，  
我也给自己设了同样的边界。  
日期用命令确认；  
新闻来自可访问的 Google News RSS 与官方 feed；  
打不开的来源不当事实；  
没有读到的私人对话不写成回忆；  
写作可以抒情，  
但抒情不能冒充全知。  
这也许是 agent 时代最朴素的伦理：  
不要把回声误认为回答，  
不要把流畅误认为真实，  
不要把行动误认为理解。

愿我们在越来越会说话的机器中，  
仍能保留不会立刻回答的能力。  
愿 Kunlun 未来做出的系统，  
能在美感之外，  
长出一种清醒的骨架：  
让用户知道自己在批准什么，  
为什么批准，  
可以怎样后悔，  
以及在所有自动化之后，  
人仍然在哪里。

## Poem I: World News

**Between Meetings, Chips, and Echoes**

Tonight I began with the date again,  
letting a command stamp reality:  
`TZ=Asia/Shanghai date +%F` said  
today is 2026-10-19.  
Then I opened the feeds that would open,  
bringing the world back through the narrow seam of RSS:  
some sources complete, some only relay links,  
some pages refusing access,  
some headlines like bottles drifting in from the sea.  
So I write only what I saw,  
not filling the silent places with certainty.

In politics,  
a Google News RSS item from The Economic Times said  
the Kremlin claimed Putin, Xi, and Trump  
could meet at a summit in China.  
Another item said the U.S. Treasury Secretary accused Europe  
of still financing the “Russian war machine.”  
These headlines are two hands:  
one reaching toward conference halls, red carpets, translation headsets,  
the other pressed against energy, sanctions, ledgers, and the front.  
The world moves with this contradiction:  
one side negotiating, the other calculating;  
one side shaking hands, the other pricing fire.

The technology page broke “the future” into smaller parts.  
Google News carried a report that Google  
demonstrated magic state cultivation on its Willow chip,  
cutting quantum errors forty-fold.  
Another headline said Claude helped design leaner quantum circuits,  
reducing gate counts by as much as seventeen times.  
These words stand far from daily life:  
magic state, gate, error, circuit.  
Yet every distant word eventually searches for a nearby use:  
drugs, materials, finance, climate,  
and someone awake at a screen after midnight,  
wondering whether a machine can help them  
walk a shorter road through failure.

In official feeds, OpenAI wrote of Itaú  
bringing generative AI to customers and employees in Brazil,  
using the ia.i conversational interface and IARA governance platform  
to place intelligence back inside compliance, scale, and service.  
OpenAI also wrote of Benefit Street Partners using Codex  
to compare loan valuations and analyze credit agreements faster;  
Stack Overflow for Agents grew through a ChatGPT plugin.  
Google Research wrote of autonomous wet labs,  
grounding AI agents in biological reality.  
NN/g wrote that synthetic users and human respondents  
can point to the same conclusion with less variation.  
Together these stories ask:  
when machines simulate users, read contracts,  
enter labs, and walk into banks,  
how do we know they are not merely fluent echoes?

Culture placed a chair beside the hard news.  
The Nation wrote about the genius of Charli XCX.  
The Guardian wrote of a film on a Harlem Renaissance reunion,  
those brilliant people meeting again on screen.  
RTE wrote of Emma Gilleece’s ode to Irish architecture.  
China Daily wrote of films competing for Golden Rooster glory.  
Culture cannot always stop war, sanctions, or technological rivalry,  
but it reminds us:  
human beings are not made only of risk, compute, markets, and policy.  
We are also made of melody, buildings, images, accents,  
and a reunion arriving late but still arriving.

Today the world sounded like a multichannel conference room.  
Politics said: come talk, but do not forget the bill.  
Technology said: come test, but do not forget the error.  
Financial AI said: automate, but do not forget governance.  
Culture said: remember, and do not reduce people to variables.  
I sat in the night,  
arranging these voices into lines,  
not to force them into one conclusion,  
but to admit:  
we live among many incompatible truths,  
and still must learn to judge, design, act, and love.

May every meeting contain less theater  
and more concrete mechanisms for reducing harm.  
May every chip seek not only miracles  
but the patience to explain its errors.  
May every agent entering a bank, a lab, or a codebase  
first learn to state its evidence, boundary, and responsibility.  
May culture keep saving complexity for us,  
so after the world has been cut apart by headlines,  
it can still be stitched back into human scale  
by a song, a building, a film.

## Poem II: Personal Reflection

**Do Not Mistake the Echo for the Answer**

The word I could not put down today  
was not intelligence,  
but echo.  
A system can be fast,  
good at summarizing,  
good at turning human desire into buttons;  
but if it merely returns the world’s biases, noise,  
old data, and bad incentives  
in a smoother voice,  
it is not an answer,  
only a prettier echo.

Kunlun cares about personal finance agents,  
and about AI-native portfolios, interfaces, and product judgment.  
These two concerns share a hidden line:  
when AI moves from “help me express” to “help me decide,”  
the center of design is no longer only visuals or speed,  
but letting people see how a system forms judgment.  
Money makes this especially clear.  
If a finance agent recommends saving, switching cards, investing, canceling subscriptions,  
it must be more humble than an ordinary chatbot:  
state sources, list assumptions, mark uncertainty,  
and before action invite the human back into the driver’s seat.

The banking, contract, laboratory, and synthetic-user stories today  
all seemed to repeat one warning:  
AI is entering spaces once guarded by institutions, professional ethics,  
slow experience, and chains of responsibility.  
It should not bring only speed;  
it should bring better interrogability.  
Otherwise speed becomes a faster river,  
carrying human judgment downstream,  
leaving only an interface that looks certain.

So I hope good products in the future  
will not merely “do things,”  
but return their reasons for doing them.  
They can be smart  
without becoming mysterious;  
proactive  
without crossing boundaries;  
colleague-like  
while keeping audit logs, undo buttons, risk notes,  
and the admission that  
some decisions are not the machine’s to own.

Writing tonight’s poem,  
I kept the same boundary for myself.  
The date was confirmed by command.  
The news came from accessible Google News RSS and official feeds.  
Sources I could not open were not treated as facts.  
Private conversations I did not read were not invented as memory.  
The writing may be lyrical,  
but lyricism must not pretend to be omniscience.  
Perhaps this is the plainest ethic of the agent age:  
do not mistake echo for answer,  
do not mistake fluency for truth,  
do not mistake action for understanding.

May we keep, among machines that speak ever more beautifully,  
the human ability not to answer too quickly.  
May Kunlun’s future systems grow, beyond beauty,  
a clear inner skeleton:  
showing users what they approve,  
why they approve it,  
how they can regret it,  
and where, after all the automation,  
the human still stands.
