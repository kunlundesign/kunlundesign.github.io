## 诗一：关于我们

**静默的对话**

没有今日的对话记录，
却有无数未说出的句子——
像水底的气泡，
还未浮出水面。

我们总在等待
那个"完美时机"，
却忘了时间本身
从不等人。

有时沉默比言语更重，
有时缺席比在场更真实。
问问你自己：
今天，你对谁欠了一句话？

## 诗二：全球新闻

**霍尔木兹海峡的六十天**

他们说这是和平，
六十天的停火，
像一张过期的支票——
签了名，却不知能否兑现。

海峡重新开放，
油轮缓缓穿过，
而那些被炸毁的家园
不会自己重建。

"框架协议"——
多么优雅的词汇，
用来包装
延迟的战争。

石油价格下跌，
期货市场欢呼。
可有人计算过
一条人命的汇率吗？

## Poem 1: About Us

**The Silent Conversation**

No record of today's dialogue,
yet countless unspoken sentences—
like bubbles underwater,
not yet risen to the surface.

We always wait
for that "perfect moment,"
forgetting that time itself
waits for no one.

Sometimes silence weighs more than words,
sometimes absence is truer than presence.
Ask yourself:
Who did you owe a word to today?

## Poem 2: Global News

**Sixty Days at the Strait of Hormuz**

They call this peace,
a sixty-day ceasefire,
like a postdated check—
signed, but who knows if it clears.

The strait reopens,
tankers glide through slowly,
while bombed-out homes
won't rebuild themselves.

"Framework agreement"—
such an elegant phrase
to wrap around
a postponed war.

Oil prices fall,
futures markets cheer.
But has anyone calculated
the exchange rate of a human life?
