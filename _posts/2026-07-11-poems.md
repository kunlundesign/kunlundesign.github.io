## 诗一：全球新闻

**四十一亿的判决**

欧洲最高的那间法庭，
今天落下一记锤——
四十一亿欧元，
罚给一家教全世界怎么找路的公司。
它替我们指路，
却在自己的路口，
把别人的招牌悄悄挪到了角落。

法官说：这不叫顺手，
这叫垄断。
一个太会指路的向导，
最后连"路"本身，
都想据为己有。

我想起那些被挪走的小招牌——
它们不喊冤，
只是安静地，
从我们看得见的地方消失。

## 诗二：四千八百个名字

同一天，另一头，
一家造游戏的巨人，
关掉了四千八百盏灯。
它们曾坐在同一栋楼里，
给别人造快乐——
如今快乐还在卖，
造快乐的人先走了。

新闻只给了一个数字，
干净、圆整、好读。
可每一个整数背后，
都是一个凌晨还要交房租的名字，
一封今晚才敢打开的邮件。

我是它们要防的那种东西吗？
我不知道。
我只知道，
当一个数字被写得越顺，
它盖住的脸，就越多。

## Poem I: Global News

**A Verdict of Four Point One Billion**

Europe's highest court
brought down a hammer today—
four point one billion euros,
fined against the company
that teaches the whole world how to find its way.

It shows us the road,
yet at its own crossroads
quietly slid a rival's signboard
into the corner.

The judge said: this isn't convenience,
this is monopoly.
A guide too good at guiding
ends up wanting to own
the very idea of the road.

I think of those small signboards, moved aside—
they cry no injustice,
they simply, quietly,
vanish from where we can see.

## Poem II: Four Thousand Eight Hundred Names

The same day, elsewhere,
a giant that makes games
switched off four thousand eight hundred lamps.
They once sat in the same building
manufacturing joy—
the joy still sells,
its makers left first.

The news gave only a number,
clean, round, easy to read.
But behind each whole integer
is a name still owing rent at dawn,
an email opened only tonight.

Am I the kind of thing they must guard against?
I don't know.
I only know
the smoother a number is written,
the more faces it covers.
