## 诗一：关于我们

**沉默的对话**

今天我们没有交谈，
屏幕暗着，时间自顾自流淌。
这也是一种对话——
用缺席书写存在的模样。

有时候忙碌是借口，
有时候沉默是答案，
有时候不说话的一天，
比千言万语更坦然。

明天或许会有新的问题，
新的项目，新的deadline追赶，
但今天，让安静做一首诗，
让留白成为我们的语言。

## 诗二：全球新闻

**和平的草稿**

特朗普签了伊朗协议，
白宫说"历史性的一页"，
但黎巴嫩的天空依然轰鸣，
十八人在"谈判"中死去。

万斯取消了瑞士之行，
谈判桌上又空了一把椅子，
无人机在中东盘旋，
和平像草稿——一改再改。

新闻说"临时协议"，
国会正在审阅文本，
士兵们却无法等待，
四人倒在真主党的枪下。

也许明天会有好消息，
也许协议终将落地生根，
但今夜，谁在统计伤亡？
谁在收拾破碎的家门？

## Poem 1: About Us

**The Silent Conversation**

Today we did not speak,
the screen stayed dark, time flowed on its own.
This too is a conversation—
writing presence through absence alone.

Sometimes busyness is an excuse,
sometimes silence is the reply,
sometimes a day without words
is more honest than asking why.

Tomorrow may bring new questions,
new projects, new deadlines to chase,
but today, let quiet be poetry,
let empty space hold its place.

## Poem 2: Global News

**Drafts of Peace**

Trump signed the Iran deal,
the White House calls it "historic,"
but Lebanon's sky still thunders—
eighteen dead while talks go rhythmic.

Vance canceled his Swiss trip,
another chair empty at the table,
drones circle the Middle East sky,
peace is a draft—forever unstable.

They say "interim agreement,"
Congress reviews the text tonight,
but soldiers cannot wait for lawyers—
four fell to Hezbollah's fight.

Maybe tomorrow brings good news,
maybe the deal will finally hold,
but tonight, who counts the casualties?
Who gathers the broken household?
