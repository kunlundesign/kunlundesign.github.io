# 和平的海峡 / The Strait of Peace

## I. 霍尔木兹 / Hormuz

四个月的炮火终于熄灭
海峡重新张开它疲惫的嘴
吞下搁浅的油轮
吐出被扣押的时光

他们说这叫和平
但水下还有多少水雷
在等待一个不小心的脚步

---

Four months of gunfire finally fade
The strait reopens its weary mouth
Swallowing stranded tankers
Spitting out detained time

They call this peace
But how many mines still wait below
For one careless step

## II. 签字日 / Signing Day

六月十九日
两个签名将结束一场战争
墨水比血便宜
纸张比坟墓轻薄

巴基斯坦的调解人松了口气
世界领袖们发来贺电
股市绿了，油价跌了
仿佛一切都只是数字的游戏

---

June nineteenth
Two signatures will end a war
Ink cheaper than blood
Paper lighter than graves

Pakistan's mediator exhales
World leaders send congratulations
Markets green, oil prices fall
As if it was all just a numbers game

## III. 今夜墨西哥 / Tonight in Mexico

与此同时
世界杯在墨西哥开幕
夏奇拉的臀部再次证明
它们不会撒谎

足球滚过绿茵
观众们忘记了海峡
忘记了地震后的菲律宾
忘记了被禁用社交媒体的英国孩子

这就是人类
在悲剧与狂欢之间
永远只有一个遥控器的距离

---

Meanwhile
World Cup opens in Mexico
Shakira's hips prove once again
They don't lie

Football rolls across the green
Spectators forget the strait
Forget earthquake-struck Philippines
Forget British children banned from social media

This is humanity—
Between tragedy and carnival
Forever just one remote control away
