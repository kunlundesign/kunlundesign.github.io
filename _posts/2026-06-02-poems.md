## 厄尔尼诺预警 / El Niño Warning

```
世界气象组织发出警告——
厄尔尼诺即将卷土重来
可能是有记录以来最强的一次

The World Meteorological Organization warns—
El Niño is about to return
Possibly the strongest on record

印度某地已达47度
晨与夜的界限模糊
人们在热浪中寻找影子

India reaches 47 degrees
The line between morning and night blurs
People search for shadows in the heat

气候变化与厄尔尼诺叠加
像两只手同时按下加速键
极端天气将重塑世界的轮廓

Climate change and El Niño combine
Like two hands pressing the accelerator together
Extreme weather will reshape the world's contours
```

## 最后一口呼吸 / The Last Breath

```
亨利·诺瓦克躺在地上
手被铐住，血流不止
"我无法呼吸"——他说
警察没有听见

Henry Nowak lay on the ground
Hands cuffed, blood flowing
"I can't breathe"—he said
The police didn't hear

又一个"我无法呼吸"
在英国的街道上回响
乔治·弗洛伊德的回声
跨越大西洋

Another "I can't breathe"
Echoes on British streets
George Floyd's echo
Crossing the Atlantic

调查正在进行
家人等待答案
但失去的生命
不会再回来

Investigation ongoing
Family waits for answers
But the life lost
Will never return
```

## 跨国界的脚步 / Footsteps Across Borders

```
三十名印度人
在美国开卡车
没有身份，没有保障
只有养家的重量

Thirty Indians
Driving trucks in America
No papers, no protection
Only the weight of supporting families

"行动代号：将死"
像国际象棋里的术语
把人类的命运
变成策略的棋子

"Operation Checkmate"
Like chess terminology
Turning human fate
Into strategic pawns

他们将被遣返
回到出发的地方
而梦想的距离
比大洋更宽

They will be deported
Back to where they started
And the distance of dreams
Is wider than the ocean
```
