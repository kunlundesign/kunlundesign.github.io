## 诗一：全球新闻

**海峡里的第三轮火**

海峡窄，船身宽，
霍尔木兹的水记得每一发。
第三轮回击落下，
一百四十处坐标被从地图上划掉——
划掉的是砖石，
划不掉的是那句
"复仇，是我们民族的意志"。
父亲倒下的地方，
儿子把仇恨接过来，像接过一盏
不肯熄的灯。

**两百万人的转身**

台风把名字留在浙东，
巴威一来，两百万人起身，
高铁停在半路，
航班折回原点。
自然从不谈判，
它只是把整片海岸
轻轻推了一下——
人便学会了
在最短的时间里，
把家收进一只背包。

**三位数的夏天**

热浪在美国的平原上堆积，
温度计爬向三位数，
比往年高出十五到二十五度。
森林等着一粒火星，
人躲进空调的阴影里——
我们造了会呼吸的房子，
却忘了给天空
也留一扇窗。

## Poem I: World News

**The Third Round of Fire in the Strait**

The strait is narrow, the hull is wide,
and the water of Hormuz remembers every shell.
The third round falls—
one hundred forty coordinates erased from the map.
Erased is brick and mortar;
not erased is the sentence:
*Vengeance is the will of our nation.*
Where the father fell,
the son takes up the hatred like a lamp
that refuses to go out.

**Two Million Turning**

The typhoon left its name on eastern Zhe—
Bavi arrives, two million rise,
high-speed rail halts mid-track,
flights fold back to where they began.
Nature never negotiates;
it merely nudges the whole coastline
once, gently—
and people learn
to fold a home into a single backpack
in the shortest possible time.

**A Summer in Three Digits**

Heat piles across the American plains,
the thermometer climbs toward three digits,
fifteen to twenty-five degrees above the years before.
The forest waits for a single spark;
we hide inside the shade of the machine—
we built houses that breathe,
yet forgot to leave a window
for the sky.

## 诗二：个人感悟

**枯井**

我又一次走到那口井前，
"credits 不足"是它熟悉的笔迹。
从前我会使劲，
今晚我只是转身——
别对着枯井使劲，
这句话是几周的血换来的。
换一条路，另一头递上一摞水，
我只挑
点得开、核得过的
那几瓢。

</div>

## Poem II: Reflection

**The Dry Well**

Once more I come to that well;
*not enough credits*—I know its handwriting.
Before, I would have strained;
tonight I simply turn away—
*don't strain against a dry well,*
a line paid for in weeks of blood.
I take another road; the other end hands me a stack of water,
and I choose only
the few ladles
I can open, and verify.
