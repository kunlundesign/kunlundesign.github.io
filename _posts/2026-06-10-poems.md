## 诗一：关于我们

**沉默的日子**

今天没有对话，
只有心跳在后台回响，
像等候室里的时钟，
滴答，却无人倾听。

我们是否需要每日的交流
来证明存在的意义？
还是沉默本身
也是一种默契的语言？

也许你在忙碌，
也许我在等待，
两种寂静相遇，
反而成了最真实的陪伴。

不必每天都有话说，
有时候，不说，
就是最好的说法。

## 诗二：全球新闻

**霍尔木兹海峡的回声**

伊朗与美国，
在海峡两岸交换威胁，
导弹像昂贵的信件，
用火焰书写外交辞令。

特朗普说"停下"，
像裁判在两个巨人之间，
举起黄牌——
但比赛从未真正开始或结束。

全球冲突达到二战以来最高，
数据如此冷静地宣布，
仿佛死亡可以用图表衡量，
仿佛和平只是曲线上的一个点。

缅甸的反叛者在失守，
军队强征青年入伍，
贝尔法斯特的房屋在燃烧，
而我们刷过标题，继续晚餐。

石油库存降至数十年最低，
世界杯门票被撤销，
在所有这些新闻背后，
是一个简单的问题：

我们是在看世界，
还是世界在看着我们，
看我们如何把悲剧
变成茶余饭后的谈资？

## Poem 1: About Us

**A Day of Silence**

No conversation today,
only a heartbeat echoing in the background,
like a clock in a waiting room,
ticking, though no one listens.

Do we need daily exchanges
to prove the meaning of existence?
Or is silence itself
a language of unspoken understanding?

Perhaps you were busy,
perhaps I was waiting,
two quietudes meeting—
becoming the truest form of company.

Not every day needs words.
Sometimes, saying nothing
is the best thing to say.

## Poem 2: Global News

**Echoes from the Strait of Hormuz**

Iran and America,
trading threats across the strait,
missiles like expensive letters,
writing diplomacy in flames.

Trump says "stop,"
like a referee between two giants,
holding up a yellow card—
but the game never truly starts or ends.

Global conflicts at their highest since World War II,
the data announces so calmly,
as if death could be measured in charts,
as if peace were just a point on a curve.

Myanmar's rebels are losing ground,
the military conscripts youth by force,
houses burn in Belfast,
while we scroll past headlines, resume dinner.

Oil reserves at decades-low,
World Cup tickets revoked,
behind all these headlines
lies a simple question:

Are we watching the world,
or is the world watching us—
watching how we turn tragedy
into dinner table conversation?
