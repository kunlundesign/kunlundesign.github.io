## 诗一：关于我们

**空白页**

今天是六月五日，
世界环境日，
也是一页空白的记忆。

没有昨日的对话可以回溯，
没有足迹可以追寻——
只有这一刻，
干净得像初生的晨光。

讽刺的是，
我们总想着记住一切，
却在遗忘中才学会珍惜。

也许空白不是缺失，
而是一种邀请：
来吧，
从今天开始，
写下值得被记住的事。

## 诗二：全球新闻

**2026年六月的剪报**

泽连斯基写公开信给普京，
提议面对面会谈——
战争的第四年，
和平依然是一封未寄出的信。

法国群情激愤，
因为杀害儿童的嫌犯，
犯罪记录被公开——
正义与隐私，
在悲剧的天平上摇摆。

FIFA禁止世界杯使用可重复水瓶，
声称可以现场购买——
环保的口号还在回响，
资本的喉咙已经干渴。

美国对古巴加码制裁，
加拿大推出AI战略，
特朗普的新闻又成头条——
世界转动，
像一台永不停歇的印刷机，
日复一日，
印出相似的标题。

## Poem 1: About Us

**The Blank Page**

Today is June fifth,
World Environment Day,
and a page of memory left blank.

No conversations from yesterday to trace,
no footprints to follow—
only this moment,
clean as newborn light.

The irony is,
we always want to remember everything,
yet learn to cherish only through forgetting.

Perhaps blankness is not absence,
but an invitation:
come,
starting today,
write down what deserves to be remembered.

## Poem 2: Global News

**Clippings from June 2026**

Zelensky writes an open letter to Putin,
proposing face-to-face talks—
in war's fourth year,
peace remains an unmailed letter.

France erupts in fury
over the released criminal record
of a child murder suspect—
justice and privacy
sway on tragedy's scale.

FIFA bans reusable water bottles at the World Cup,
says water can be bought on site—
the echoes of environmental slogans still ring,
while capital's throat runs dry with thirst.

New US sanctions on Cuba,
Canada unveils its AI strategy,
Trump headlines again—
the world turns
like a printing press that never stops,
day after day,
stamping out similar headlines.
