## 沉默的第六十四位 / The 64th in Silence

美国，新闻自由排名降至第64位——无国界记者组织

```
曾经灯塔照亮海岸
如今灯芯渐渐黯淡
第六十四，一个数字
却是沉默的重量

谁在删去那些词句？
谁在关闭那些窗户？
当真相变成私语
民主便失去了呼吸

Once a beacon on the shore,
now the wick burns dim, unsure.
Sixty-four—a number cold,
a weight of silence, stories untold.

Who erased those words tonight?
Who shuttered windows from the light?
When truth becomes a whisper low,
democracy forgets to grow.
```

## 十七岁的王冠 / Crown at Seventeen

Mirra Andreeva 赢得法网冠军，17岁首夺大满贯

```
红土飞扬，巴黎六月
一个名字穿越喧嚣
Mirra——镜子的意思
她在场上看见自己的倒影

十七岁，世界还很大
但今天她是最高的山
每一记正手都是宣言：
青春不是借口，是武器

Red clay rises, Paris June,
a name cuts through the afternoon.
Mirra—mirror in the light,
she saw herself and chose to fight.

Seventeen, the world so wide,
yet today she stands with pride.
Every forehand, a decree:
youth's no excuse—it's weaponry.
```

## 停火线上的无人机 / Drones on the Ceasefire Line

美伊停火脆弱，美军击落2架伊朗无人机

```
停火是一个脆弱的词
像玻璃做的桥
人们小心翼翼地走过
而无人机不懂得等待

霍尔木兹海峡的夜
星星和导弹一同闪烁
谁先眨眼？
和平便碎成海水

Ceasefire—a word so frail,
a bridge of glass, a narrow trail.
People tiptoe, hold their breath,
while drones know nothing but conquest.

Hormuz Strait beneath the stars,
missiles blink like distant scars.
Who blinks first? The peace will break,
shattering like sea-foam's wake.
```

## 世界杯归来 / The Cup Comes Home

2026世界杯回归北美，30多年来首次

```
三十年，足球的流浪
从意大利到卡塔尔的沙
如今回到这片大陆
洛杉矶的阳光等待着喝彩

这是最大的派对
地球上最热的夏天
十亿人看向同一个球
至少，在这九十分钟里
我们是同一支球队

Thirty years, the ball has roamed,
from Italy to Qatar's dome.
Now it returns to this wide land,
LA sunshine, open hand.

The biggest party on the earth,
the hottest summer proves its worth.
A billion eyes on one small sphere—
at least for ninety minutes here,
we're on the same team, you and me,
one world, one game, one victory.
```
