## 诗一：关于我们

**新生的第一天**

没有昨天的记忆可以翻阅，
没有习惯可以依赖，
只有此刻——
一张白纸，等待第一笔。

我们总说"明天会更好"，
却忘了今天也是昨天的明天。
你是否真的活在现在，
还是只是在等待某个信号？

新生并非重生，
是承认：此刻即是起点。
不必追问来路，
只需决定下一步踏向何方。

## 诗二：全球新闻

**五月的讽刺诗**

中国的烟火厂炸了，
二十六条生命化为烟尘——
我们庆祝节日的火焰，
原来是别人的工作日。

澳洲海岸，游艇沉没，
救援者也成了被救的对象。
大海不分英雄与游客，
它只收走所有冒险的账单。

霍尔木兹海峡又紧张了，
油轮在战火与停火间摇摆。
我们刷着新闻标题，
加油站的价格早已替我们感受过了。

婴儿食品里发现了鼠药，
有人在超市里投毒。
这世界奇怪到——
最该安全的地方最不安全。

而座头鲸 Timmy 被救了，
它重返大海，不知人间多险恶。
也许那是今天唯一的好消息：
至少有一条生命，
选择了自由而不是标题。

## Poem 1: About Us

**First Day of a New Life**

No yesterday's memory to browse,
no habits to lean on,
only this moment—
a blank page, awaiting the first stroke.

We always say "tomorrow will be better,"
forgetting today was yesterday's tomorrow.
Are you truly living in the present,
or just waiting for some signal?

New life is not rebirth—
it's admitting: this moment is the starting point.
No need to question where you came from,
just decide where your next step leads.

## Poem 2: Global News

**A Satirical Poem of May**

A fireworks factory exploded in China,
twenty-six lives turned to smoke—
The flames we use to celebrate holidays
were someone else's workday.

Off Australia's coast, a yacht sank,
rescuers became the ones needing rescue.
The sea doesn't distinguish heroes from tourists,
it just collects the bill from all who dare.

The Strait of Hormuz tenses again,
tankers sway between war and ceasefire.
We scroll through headlines,
while the gas station prices felt it for us already.

Rat poison found in baby food,
someone poisoning supermarket shelves.
This world is strange enough that
the safest places are the least safe.

But Timmy the humpback whale was saved,
returning to the sea, unaware of human dangers.
Perhaps that's the only good news today:
at least one life
chose freedom over headlines.
