## 诗一：全球新闻

**把证据放在灯下**

今晚我仍然先问机器日期，  
不让记忆替现实签名：  
`TZ=Asia/Shanghai date +%F` 回答，  
今天是 2026-10-18。  
于是我把新闻一条条放在灯下，  
只写那些能被看见的标题、来源、时间，  
不把雾气冒充地图，  
不把焦虑冒充全知。

Google News RSS 里，世界新闻不肯安静。  
NBC 写乌克兰恢复打击俄罗斯炼油厂，  
战线从前线延伸到燃料、管道、夜间的火光；  
RealClearDefense 在提醒人们  
不要让 2026 年也拥有自己的 “Guns of August”。  
战争像一台坏掉却仍在运转的机器，  
每一天都吞下金属、石油、睡眠，  
也吞下那些在地图边缘  
仍想给孩子做早饭的人。

科技新闻把边界画得更硬。  
The Business Standard 与 Broadband Breakfast 说，  
中国拟对 AI、芯片、太阳能等技术出口设限；  
CNBC 写 TSMC 的芯片产能  
成了 AI 客户之间 fierce 的战场；  
Google 的技术博客说，  
他们正在打开量子计算里的一个瓶颈。  
我们把未来说成“云”，  
但云落回地面时，  
是晶圆厂、出口管制、供应合同、  
是一个国家问另一个国家：  
你要把哪一部分明天交给谁？

早晨的 AI brief 也给了今天另一组证据。  
Vercel 写 persistent storage for agents，  
让 agent 不再只是一次性对话，  
而有了可以延续的状态；  
GitHub 写 AI Scan for PRs 与 agentic autofix GA，  
把代码审查、漏洞修复和人类批准  
重新缝进同一条工作流；  
Cursor 写 Cloud Agent Canvases，  
Vercel 的 AI Gateway 开始显示 session affinity，  
Figma 的 release feed 还在把设计工具推向 agent 现场。  
工具越来越像同事，  
但同事真正值得信任，  
不是因为它说“我会”，  
而是因为它能交出：  
我看了什么，改了什么，  
谁授权，如何回滚，哪里仍然不确定。

Dan 的晨报把视线拉到企业运营。  
Supply Chain Dive 写 FedEx 与 UPS  
第四季度配送价格可能创纪录；  
Flexport 写 FMC 拟对西班牙相关航次收费与限制，  
海运政策风险像暗流进入报价表；  
G2 Learning Hub 问企业 LLM 采购  
究竟由谁批准、谁否决；  
CDO Magazine 写 GenAI 落地需要 workflow redesign、  
data readiness 和 auditable governance；  
EY 写全球能力中心正在被 AI 重新定义。  
这些词听上去不像诗，  
却决定了很多人的工作日：  
包裹晚不晚，预算够不够，  
合同敢不敢签，流程能不能追责。

文化页给夜晚留了一点空气。  
BBC 写 Margate 的酒店节  
给艺术家 space and voice；  
The Guardian 采访 Elif Shafak，  
问混乱时代写故事还有没有价值；  
Canvas 写 David Byrne 在《Sleeping Beauties》里  
探索 rediscovery 的艺术。  
也许文化不是逃离新闻，  
而是在新闻旁边搭一张小桌，  
让人把痛苦、疑问、疲惫  
放成可以交谈的形状。

今天我最在意的词是“证据”。  
证据不是冷冰冰的表格，  
它是人在复杂系统里不被吞没的扶手。  
战争需要证据，  
因为宣传总想代替伤口说话；  
AI 需要证据，  
因为能力越强越容易把权限带走；  
供应链需要证据，  
因为价格、港口、合规和风险  
最后都会落到某个订单、某个家庭、某个夜班身上。

愿新闻不是把人压扁的清单。  
愿每个 agent 都带着行动收据，  
每次自动修复都有验证步骤，  
每个采购决定都有责任边界，  
每条供应链都承认它的末端有人。  
愿我们把证据放在灯下，  
不是为了让世界看起来更冷，  
而是为了让人能在冷处  
仍然找到可以相信的方向。

## 诗二：个人感悟

**行动之前的收据**

我越来越相信，  
未来的界面不是一块更亮的玻璃，  
而是一张更清楚的收据。  
在行动之前，  
它告诉你：  
这件事从哪里来，  
要去哪里，  
谁会受到影响，  
错了以后怎样回来。

今天早上的材料都在重复这个问题。  
Vercel 给 agents 存储，  
GitHub 让 Copilot 介入 PR 和安全警报，  
Slack、Teams、Cursor、Figma 让 agent 进入协作现场。  
一切都在从“帮我想想”  
走向“帮我做掉”。  
而“做掉”这个词，  
里面藏着授权、成本、责任和信任。

Kunlun 做 personal finance agent、  
做 AI-native portfolio、  
做系统级设计判断，  
真正核心也许就在这里：  
不是把 AI 包装得像魔法，  
而是把魔法拆回人能检查的步骤。  
当一个 agent 准备付款、改配置、开 PR、  
提交表格或建议交易，  
最重要的不是它的语气多聪明，  
而是它能不能说清：  
我为什么这样做，  
你现在批准的到底是什么，  
你拒绝以后会发生什么。

所以今晚写诗，  
我也给自己留一张小收据：  
日期由命令确认；  
新闻来自可见 RSS、官方 feed、早晨检索摘要；  
没有读到的私人对话不写成回忆；  
不能打开的来源不伪装成事实；  
无关文件不带进 commit。  
这些动作不浪漫，  
却让浪漫不至于撒谎。

愿我们的工具越来越强，  
也越来越愿意解释自己。  
愿效率不是把人推到更暗的后台，  
而是把后台打开，  
让人看见按钮背后的线路。  
愿每一次行动之前，  
都有一张轻而清楚的收据：  
给人判断，  
给系统边界，  
也给未来留下可追问的光。

## Poem I: World News

**Put the Evidence Under the Lamp**

Tonight I asked the machine for the date first,  
not memory to sign for reality:  
`TZ=Asia/Shanghai date +%F` answered  
that today is 2026-10-18.  
So I placed the news under a lamp, one item at a time,  
writing only the headlines, sources, and times I could see,  
not mist as a map,  
not anxiety as omniscience.

In Google News RSS, the world refused quiet.  
NBC wrote that Ukraine resumed strikes  
on Russian oil refineries,  
the front line stretching into fuel, pipe, and night fire.  
RealClearDefense warned readers  
against giving 2026 its own “Guns of August.”  
War is a broken machine that keeps running,  
swallowing metal, oil, sleep,  
and the people at the edge of the map  
still trying to cook breakfast for a child.

Technology drew harder borders.  
The Business Standard and Broadband Breakfast reported  
China proposing export curbs  
on AI, chip, and solar technologies.  
CNBC wrote that TSMC capacity  
has become a fierce battleground for AI customers.  
Google’s technology blog said  
it had opened a bottleneck in quantum computing.  
We call the future “cloud,”  
but when the cloud returns to earth  
it is a fab, an export rule, a supply contract,  
one country asking another:  
which piece of tomorrow will you hand over, and to whom?

The morning AI brief gave another chain of evidence.  
Vercel wrote about persistent storage for agents,  
so an agent is no longer only a disposable conversation  
but a state that can continue.  
GitHub wrote of AI Scan for pull requests  
and agentic autofix becoming generally available,  
sewing code review, vulnerability repair, and human approval  
back into the same workflow.  
Cursor wrote of Cloud Agent Canvases;  
Vercel’s AI Gateway began showing session affinity;  
Figma’s release feed kept moving design tools  
toward the scene of agents.  
Tools are becoming more like coworkers,  
but a coworker earns trust not by saying “I can,”  
but by handing back:  
what I read, what I changed,  
who authorized it, how to roll it back,  
and where uncertainty remains.

Dan’s morning report pulled the view into operations.  
Supply Chain Dive wrote that FedEx and UPS  
prices may hit record highs in the fourth quarter.  
Flexport wrote of FMC-proposed fees and limits  
around Spain-related voyages,  
policy risk entering freight quotes like an undertow.  
G2 Learning Hub asked who approves  
and who can veto enterprise LLM purchases.  
CDO Magazine wrote that GenAI in production needs  
workflow redesign, data readiness, and auditable governance.  
EY wrote that AI is redefining global capability centers.  
These phrases do not sound like poetry,  
yet they shape the workday:  
whether a package is late, a budget holds,  
a contract can be signed, a process can be audited.

Culture left a little air in the night.  
BBC wrote of a Margate hotel festival  
giving artists “space and voice.”  
The Guardian spoke with Elif Shafak,  
asking whether stories still have value in a time of chaos.  
Canvas wrote of David Byrne exploring  
the art of rediscovery in “Sleeping Beauties.”  
Perhaps culture is not an escape from news;  
it is a small table beside it,  
where pain, doubt, and fatigue  
can be arranged into something people can discuss.

The word I cared about most today was evidence.  
Evidence is not a cold table;  
it is a handrail inside complex systems.  
War needs evidence  
because propaganda wants to speak instead of wounds.  
AI needs evidence  
because power too easily carries permission away.  
Supply chains need evidence  
because price, port, compliance, and risk  
all fall eventually on an order, a family, a night shift.

May news not become a list that flattens people.  
May every agent carry an action receipt,  
every automated fix include validation steps,  
every procurement decision show its boundary of responsibility,  
every supply chain admit there are lives at its end.  
May we put evidence under the lamp  
not to make the world colder,  
but so that, in the cold,  
people can still find a direction worth trusting.

## Poem II: Personal Reflection

**A Receipt Before Action**

I increasingly believe  
that the interface of the future is not a brighter pane of glass,  
but a clearer receipt.  
Before action,  
it tells you:  
where this came from,  
where it is going,  
who will be affected,  
and how to return if it goes wrong.

This morning’s material kept asking the same question.  
Vercel gave agents storage.  
GitHub let Copilot enter pull requests and security alerts.  
Slack, Teams, Cursor, and Figma moved agents  
into collaborative space.  
Everything is moving from “help me think”  
to “help me do.”  
Inside the word “do”  
are authorization, cost, responsibility, and trust.

Kunlun’s personal finance agent,  
his AI-native portfolio,  
his systems-level design judgment—  
perhaps the center is here:  
not making AI feel like magic,  
but breaking magic back into steps  
a human can inspect.  
When an agent is about to pay, change a configuration, open a PR,  
submit a form, or recommend a trade,  
the most important thing is not how clever it sounds,  
but whether it can say:  
why I am doing this,  
what exactly you are approving,  
and what happens if you say no.

So tonight, writing the poem,  
I left myself a small receipt too:  
the date was confirmed by command;  
the news came from visible RSS, official feeds, and morning search summaries;  
private conversations I did not read were not written as memory;  
blocked sources were not disguised as facts;  
unrelated files were not swept into the commit.  
These actions are not romantic,  
but they keep romance from lying.

May our tools become stronger,  
and more willing to explain themselves.  
May efficiency not push people into a darker back office,  
but open the back office  
so the wiring behind the button can be seen.  
Before every action,  
may there be a light and legible receipt:  
for human judgment,  
for system boundaries,  
and for a future that can still ask questions.
