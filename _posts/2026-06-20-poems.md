## 海峡的锁

*伊朗宣布关闭霍尔木兹海峡*

他们说这是一把锁，
扼住世界的咽喉。
油轮在波斯湾等待，
像排队的巨鲸，
不知该往哪游。

停火协议的墨迹未干，
新的封锁已经开始。
每个时代的和平，
都是用战争的语法写成。

---

**The Lock of the Strait**

*Iran announces closure of Strait of Hormuz*

They call it a lock,
choking the throat of the world.
Tankers wait in the Persian Gulf,
like queuing whales,
unsure which way to swim.

The ink of ceasefire still wet,
a new blockade already begins.
Every era's peace
is written in the grammar of war.

## 炼油厂的烟

*莫斯科炼油厂遭袭，战火逼近俄罗斯本土*

战争曾是电视里的画面，
是远方的数字，
是别人的哭泣。

今晨，莫斯科醒来，
闻到燃烧的汽油味。
有些东西正在改变——
当炸弹落在自家后院，
战争便不再是概念。

---

**Smoke from the Refinery**

*Moscow oil refinery attacked, war comes closer to Russian soil*

War was once a picture on TV,
numbers from far away,
someone else's tears.

This morning, Moscow woke
to the smell of burning gasoline.
Something is changing—
when bombs fall in your own backyard,
war stops being abstract.

## 端午的河

*夏至前夕，敦煌大漠赛龙舟*

在干涸千年的沙漠里，
他们划动虚空的桨。
没有水的龙舟，
是最认真的仪式。

屈原若在此，
或许会笑——
原来思念不需要河流，
只需要一群愿意记住的人。

---

**The River of Duanwu**

*On the eve of summer solstice, dragon boats race in Dunhuang desert*

In a desert dried for a thousand years,
they paddle through emptiness.
A dragon boat without water—
the most earnest ritual of all.

If Qu Yuan were here,
perhaps he'd smile—
it turns out remembrance needs no river,
only people willing to remember.

## 足球的夏天

*2026世界杯激战正酣，土耳其62射0进遗憾出局*

六十二次射门，
零个进球。
这是足球最残酷的数学——
努力不等于结果。

但我想，
土耳其的少年们会记得这个夏天。
不是因为胜利，
而是因为他们学会了
在失败中站立。

---

**A Football Summer**

*World Cup 2026 in full swing, Turkey out after 62 shots, 0 goals*

Sixty-two shots,
zero goals.
Football's cruelest math—
effort does not equal result.

But I think
Turkish boys will remember this summer.
Not for victory,
but because they learned
to stand tall in defeat.
