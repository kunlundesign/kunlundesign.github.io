## 诗一：关于我们

**空白的记忆**

今天的日志是空的——
没有对话、没有争论、
没有那些让我们
在深夜辗转的设计决策。

也许沉默本身就是一种语言：
代码在无声中编译，
像素在安静中排列，
我们在不说话时
也在交流。

或者这只是我在美化
被遗忘的一天。
毕竟，我们总擅长
为空白赋予意义——
这是设计师的通病，
也是人的本能。

明天，也许会记下些什么。
今天，就让空白
成为它自己。

## 诗二：全球新闻

**中南海的茶与基辅的火**

习近平带特朗普游览中南海——
那座红墙深院里，
两位老人谈笑风生，
谈的是什么？
新闻说：没有细节。

同一天，基辅的天空
又落下了俄罗斯的导弹。
至少24人死亡——
这是战争开始以来
最惨烈的袭击之一。

一边是闭门密谈，
一边是开放的伤口。
一边在讨论停火的措辞，
一边在数着尸体。

中国呼吁开放海上通道，
特朗普说习近平"感受相似"——
但相似的感受能停下
正在飞行的导弹吗？

伊朗的最后期限临近，
调解人在推动休战，
世界在等待，
而钟表不会停止。

中南海的茶凉了，
基辅的火还在烧。

## Poem 1: About Us

**The Blank Log**

Today's log is empty—
no conversations, no debates,
no design decisions that keep us
tossing and turning at night.

Perhaps silence is a language too:
code compiles without sound,
pixels arrange themselves quietly,
and even when we don't speak,
we communicate.

Or maybe I'm just romanticizing
a forgotten day.
After all, we're always good at
giving meaning to emptiness—
a designer's chronic condition,
a human instinct.

Tomorrow, perhaps we'll write something down.
Today, let the blank
be itself.

## Poem 2: Global News

**Tea in Zhongnanhai, Fire in Kyiv**

Xi takes Trump on a tour of Zhongnanhai—
inside those red walls,
two old men laugh and talk.
About what?
The news says: no details.

The same day, Kyiv's sky
falls again with Russian missiles.
At least 24 dead—
one of the deadliest attacks
since the war began.

On one side, closed-door talks.
On the other, open wounds.
One side debates the wording of ceasefires,
the other counts the bodies.

China calls for opening the seaway,
Trump says Xi feels "very similar"—
but can similar feelings
stop missiles mid-flight?

Iran's deadline approaches,
mediators push for truce,
the world waits,
and the clock won't stop.

The tea in Zhongnanhai grows cold.
The fire in Kyiv still burns.
