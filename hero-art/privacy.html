Privacy Policy

Hero Art / 将图 · Effective September 19, 2026

Local gameplay

Hero Art is developed by Kunlun Liu. No developer account or login is required. Puzzle progress, collected portraits, badges and preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not send this gameplay data to a developer-operated server and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Purchases and Apple services

In-app purchases and restoration are handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app reads verified purchase entitlements to unlock content. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings. TestFlight feedback and diagnostics are governed by Apple's testing service.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and investigate your request, not for advertising. Correspondence is retained only as needed for support and any applicable obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the address below, subject to those obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

Sharing and external links

Sharing is initiated by you through the system share sheet. The service you choose handles the shared content under its own policy. Historical references open external websites, which have their own privacy practices.

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement. These pages do not add analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Your choices and contact

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app; this may permanently remove progress. Purchase records held by Apple are managed separately. For privacy questions or support-data requests, contact Kunlun Liu at liukunlun@outlook.com. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated effective date.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 19 日。开发者：Kunlun Liu。

《将图》无需注册或登录开发者账户。拼图进度、收藏、徽章与偏好保存在设备本地，不发送至开发者运营的服务器。App 不含广告或第三方分析 SDK。设备备份是否包含 App 数据取决于你的 Apple 设置。

购买与恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理；App 读取经验证的购买权益以解锁内容。开发者不会收到银行卡信息。Apple 可能根据其政策及你的设置处理交易、诊断或 TestFlight 反馈。

主动发送支持邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱及你提供的内容，仅用于回复和排查问题，不用于广告。邮件仅在支持需求及适用义务所需期间保留。你可联系下方邮箱请求删除，适用义务要求保留的情况除外。

分享由你通过系统分享菜单发起，接收服务适用其自己的隐私政策。历史资料链接指向第三方网站。本支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能处理 IP 地址等请求信息；本站未添加分析脚本或广告 Cookie。

删除 App 可移除本地 App 数据，也可能永久丢失进度；Apple 的购买记录另行管理。隐私问题或支持邮件删除请求请联系 liukunlun@outlook.com。政策变更将在本页更新生效日期。
