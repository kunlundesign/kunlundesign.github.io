Purchases

Ten selected portraits are free. The optional Complete Edition is a one-time purchase that unlocks puzzle play for 45 additional portraits in the 55-portrait edition. Future separate expansions are not included. The price shown in Apple's purchase sheet is the price for your storefront.

To restore an existing purchase, open the Complete Edition purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. An internet connection is required. Do not buy it again to troubleshoot a restoration problem.

Progress and replay

Puzzle progress and collected portraits are stored on your device. Completed puzzles can be replayed from All Puzzles. Deleting the app may remove your local progress. Restoring a purchase restores access, not puzzle progress.

Report a problem

Email the portrait name, difficulty, app version, device model, iOS version and steps that caused the problem. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

Purchase refunds are handled by Apple: request help with an Apple purchase.

购买与恢复

游戏提供 10 张精选画像免费试玩。完整版为一次性购买，解锁本版 55 张画像中另外 45 张的拼图游玩权限，不包含未来独立扩展包。实际价格以 Apple 购买确认页显示为准。

如已购买，请使用原购买 Apple 账户，在完整版购买页点击“恢复购买”，并保持网络连接。无需为了排查恢复问题重复购买。

存档与重复游玩

拼图进度与收藏保存在设备本地。已完成的拼图可以从“所有拼图”再次游玩。删除 App 可能丢失本地存档；恢复购买仅恢复解锁权限，不恢复拼图进度。

请将武将名称、难度、App 版本、设备型号、iOS 版本及复现步骤发送至 liukunlun@outlook.com，可附截图。请勿发送密码、验证码或银行卡信息。
