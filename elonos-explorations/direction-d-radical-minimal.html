How I Think
Four principles
Subtraction is a feature.
Every element should justify its presence. When in doubt, it goes.
Systems over screens.
Design the rules and the loops. The pixels follow from a sound model.
Taste is a decision, not a vibe.
I can name why something is right. If I can't, it isn't finished.
Ship the judgment, not the demo.
The point is a product that holds up on day 100, not a pretty first run.
